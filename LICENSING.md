Licensing a Containerfile under GPL does **not** relicense the operating system's upstream packages.

## Name, logos and visual branding

The Openfen name, logo, wordmark, and original visual assets under `image/branding/logos/` and `image/branding/wallpapers/` are **not included in the GPL-3.0-or-later grant for software source files**. Their redistribution and modification terms have not yet been published as a separate artwork license. Do not assume that a software licence grants permission to market a modified distribution as an official Openfen release, or to use Openfen marks as an endorsement.

Before making public, redistributable Openfen images, maintainers should establish an explicit licence for original artwork and a clear policy governing use of Openfen marks. Any branding policy must be consistent with the applicable licences of the individual components.
